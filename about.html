About us

Independent, local, and personal since 2014.

Premier Eye Institute is a doctor-owned practice — no corporate playbook, just careful eye care and honest advice for our neighbors in Creedmoor.

Your doctor

Dr. Nisha Mehta, OD

Dr. Mehta founded Premier Eye Institute in 2014 and has examined more than 5,000 eyes in Creedmoor since. Her training spans clinical eye disease, academia, and mission work — and it shows in exams that are thorough, calm, and clearly explained.

  • Doctor of Optometry, Pennsylvania College of Optometry (2006)

  • Residency in ocular disease, Baltimore VA Medical Center

  • Fellow of the American Academy of Optometry

  • Former clinical assistant professor, UNC Chapel Hill

  • MBA, East Carolina University

  • Eye care mission work in Guatemala

What to expect

Your first visit, step by step.

Check-in

Check-in

A warm welcome from the front desk, a few minutes of paperwork if you're new, and quick pre-testing to map how your eyes are doing today.

The exam

The exam

Unhurried time with Dr. Mehta — modern imaging and screening, honest answers, and a prescription you can trust.

The optical

The optical

If you need eyewear, our optical team helps you find frames that fit your face, your prescription, and your budget — then adjusts them until they're right.

After your visit

After your visit

You leave with a clear plan for your eyes. Between visits, we're a phone call away — and existing patients can use the Crystal PM portal.
